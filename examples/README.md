# Examples — screens you consider excellent

Drop 3–5 screenshots of iOS screens you love into this folder (PNG, JPG or WebP). Good sources: your own phone's screenshots of apps you admire, or shots you saved from Dribbble/Mobbin for personal reference.

Then add a line for each in `notes.md` saying what to learn from it — that's what makes examples useful:

```
habit-home.png — love the huge rounded numbers and how little color there is
music-player.png — background tinted from artwork, controls with no backgrounds
banking-list.png — perfect row density, amounts right-aligned in tabular numbers
```

Quality over quantity: 5 great, Apple-quality examples teach more than 50 random ones, and concept shots that ignore iOS conventions will pull designs away from feeling native.

The skill also learns from your own approved designs: when you love a mockup it made, copy its PNG here with a note.
