# Example notes

35 reference screens the owner loves (20 JPG, 15 WebP). Read this file first, then open **only the 2–3 images that match the current design**. Opening all of them wastes context.

The WebP files are small thumbnails (~410 px wide). Use them for overall mood and composition only. For spacing and type detail, use the JPGs.

## What these examples say about the owner's taste
- **Dark-first.** About 28 of 35 are dark: near-black backgrounds (#000–#121212) with slightly lifted cards (#1C1C1E / #2C2C2E).
- **One vivid accent on dark.** Neon lime (#D4FF3A-ish), warm orange, indigo/violet or electric blue. It's used for the selected state, the hero tile and the main action, never everywhere.
- **Numbers are the hero.** Balances, steps, streaks and scores are set 40–72 pt bold. Decimals and units are smaller and grayer ("$3,200.00", "190 lbs", "2h 26m").
- **Bento grids.** 2-column stat tiles (number + small label + optional icon) with varied heights. One tile is often filled with the accent.
- **Streak heatmaps and dot grids.** These are a recurring love: GitHub-style day grids for habits, journaling and activity.
- **Pill everything.** Pill segmented controls (Day/Week/Month) with an accent-filled selection, pill chips, floating pill tab bars with a filled selected tab, circular icon buttons.
- **Soft ambient glow.** A blurred color glow from the top or behind the hero (orange, teal, lime), fading into black.
- **Charts are friendly.** Rounded bars, smooth lines with one highlighted point and a tooltip bubble, rings with rounded caps.
- **Geometric sans display type.** Many use Manrope, Plus Jakarta or Urbanist-style faces. Keep SF Pro for text; a rounded or geometric face for big numbers is acceptable.

## Don't copy these (they break iOS conventions)
- Hamburger menus (`(9).jpg`, `(13).jpg`) → tab bar.
- A center "+", scan or camera FAB inside the tab bar (`(9).jpg`, `(14).jpg`, `(5).jpg`, `(11).webp`) → put create/scan in the nav bar or a toolbar button.
- Left arrows "←" as back buttons → iOS back chevron in a glass circle.
- ALL-CAPS labels everywhere (`(2).jpg`, `(6).jpg`) → title/sentence case.
- Icon-only tab bars without labels (`(11).jpg`, `(15).jpg`, `(16).jpg`) → keep short labels under icons (the HIG asks for them).
- Tiny low-contrast gray text on dark → `--label-2` at ≥ 13 pt minimum for anything people need to read.
- Big "Hello, Name 👋" greeting blocks pushing content down → a small greeting line is fine; lead with the hero number.
